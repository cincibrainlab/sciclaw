Quick Start

Skills are built-in helpers for common research tasks. You do not need to choose one before you begin.

In Discord Webchat, describe what you want in plain language. For example: “Find recent papers on this topic,” “make a figure,” or “help revise this paragraph.” sciClaw chooses the right helper when one is available. If you prefer to choose yourself, type /skill in Discord and select from the suggested list.

Available Skills

Research and Reading

  • scientific-writing — Help draft or improve a manuscript section.
  • pubmed-cli — Find papers in PubMed and look up their details and related work.
  • biorxiv-database — Find and summarize recent bioRxiv and medRxiv preprints.

Writing and Figures

  • quarto-authoring — Help write and prepare Quarto documents.
  • pandoc-docx — Turn a draft into a Word document.
  • imagemagick — Resize, crop, convert, or combine research images.
  • beautiful-mermaid — Make clear diagrams for papers and presentations.
  • explainer-site — Create a simple web page that explains a topic.
  • image-generation — Create images and visuals for a presentation or document.

Keeping a Clear Record

  • experiment-provenance — Keep a record of how an experiment was run and what it produced.
  • benchmark-logging — Record how well a method performed and how it was evaluated.

Documents and Files

  • docx-review — Review and edit Word documents, including comments and tracked changes.
  • acroform-fill — Fill in PDF forms.
  • pptx — Create, edit, or summarize PowerPoint presentations.
  • pdf — Read, combine, split, convert, or pull information from PDF files.
  • xlsx — Create, clean, analyze, or convert spreadsheets.

Writing Style and Privacy

  • humanize-text — Make writing sound more natural and easier to read.
  • phi-cleaner — Remove personal health information from clinical text before sharing it.
