Discord Webchat FAQ

What is sciClaw Webchat?

sciClaw Webchat is a browser client for a sciClaw Chat deployment, based on the Spacebar client Fermi. It is separate from the sciClaw operator dashboard.

How do I get started?

Open the webchat address provided by your deployment administrator and sign in. Choose a channel where the sciClaw bot is available and describe your request, mentioning the bot if the channel requires it. Ask your administrator if you need an account or cannot access a channel.

How do I ask for help?

Ask the bot a question in plain language, or consult the sciClaw documentation. For deployment or account problems, contact your administrator.

Why did the bot not respond?

Check that you are in the correct channel and have access to the bot. Try mentioning the bot; routed channels may require it. If the problem continues, contact your administrator; the bot or its gateway may be unavailable.
